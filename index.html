Common Questions About Mental Wellness

What is ExoTMS®?

ExoMind uses FDA-cleared TMS technology powered by BTL’s patented ExoTMS™ system. It uses advanced targeting with magnetic pulses to stimulate nerve cells in key brain regions responsible for mood regulation (DLPFC). It reawakens underactive areas, restoring neural balance and promoting emotional resilience without medication. The treatment is generally well tolerated.

How long does each session take?

ExoMind is administered in a comfortable, non-invasive 30-minute session. You can relax, read, or listen to music while the targeted brain stimulation enhances your mood and clarity.

Is ExoMind covered by insurance?

Depending on your provider and individual healthcare requirements, ExoMind may be eligible for insurance coverage. If you are looking for mental clinics near me that offer cutting-edge care, schedule a consultation at our partner clinic to determine your specific coverage options.

How soon will I feel results?

Many patients report feeling mentally stronger after their first ExoMind session. However, for most, a complete treatment plan is best to feel the full spectrum of mental wellness results. Individual results vary depending on body, health, and mental state.
